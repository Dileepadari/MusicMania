Music makes your life beautiful

Music Mania is a review site for albums, artists and tracks. It exists to make one thing easy: find out whether something is worth listening to, then say what you thought of it.

{% with title = 'What you can do here' %}{% include "partials/_section_head.html" %}{% endwith %}

Browse the catalogue

Top artists, albums and songs on the home page, and the full artist list with ratings, languages and awards.

Rate and review

Give any artist or album a rating out of five and write a review. Reviews stay with the thing they were written about.

Keep a playlist

Add any track to your playlist from the home page or an album page, and manage it all from one table.

Search and preview

Search the iTunes catalogue for anything not in ours, filter by duration, and play a 30 second preview.

{% with title = 'How to use it' %}{% include "partials/_section_head.html" %}{% endwith %}
  1. Log in, or create an account if this is your first visit.
  2. Start at the home page for the highest rated artists, albums and songs.
  3. Open an artist to see their albums, then open an album to see its tracklist.
  4. Use the breadcrumb trail under the navigation bar to get back up a level.
  5. Use the Add button on any track to put it in your playlist.
  6. Leave a rating and a review on the artist and album pages.
